## What we store

Your e-mail address (for your account) and your progress: chapters read, prayers marked as prayed, reading-plan days, quiz results, bookmarks, highlights, notes and favourite prayers. This is stored on your device and, so you keep it on a new phone, in your account (hosted by Supabase in the EU).

## What we do not do

We do not sell your data, show advertising, or track you across other apps. We do not read your notes.

## Daily information

To show the saint and readings of the day, the app requests public calendars from orthocal.info, api.coptic.io or calapi.inadiutorium.cz. These requests contain only the date.

## Notifications

Prayer-time and gentle inactivity reminders are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off at any time in Me → Settings.

## Your rights

You can see, export or delete your data. Deleting your account in Me → Settings removes your account and all synced progress.

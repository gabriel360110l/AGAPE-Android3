# Privacy Policy

Draft, last updated 23 October 2026. This text describes what the app actually does, but it has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. Parts in [square brackets] still have to be filled in before publication.

## Who is responsible

AGAPE is offered by [publisher name and address], the Netherlands ("we"). We are responsible for the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us at gabriel360110l@gmail.com.

## In short

AGAPE works offline. The Bible, prayers, commentary and other content are part of the app and do not send anything anywhere. We only store what is needed for your account and your progress. We do not show advertising, do not sell your data, do not use analytics or tracking tools, and do not track you across other apps or websites.

## Your account

To keep your progress, the app asks you to create an account with your e-mail address and a password, or to sign in with Google. For an account we store your e-mail address, a user ID and the date the account was created. Your password is stored by our hosting provider only in hashed form; we cannot see it. If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your e-mail address and confirms your identity; we receive no other information from your Google account.

## Your progress

Your progress consists of: chapters you have read, days you used the app, reading plans and the days you completed, quiz results, bookmarks, highlights, your own notes, and favourite prayers. This is first saved on your device. When you are signed in, a copy is kept in your account, so that you keep it on a new phone. Your notes are part of this copy. Technical measures (row-level security) make sure that only your own account can read or change it. We do not look at your progress or notes, except when you ask us to help with a problem or when the law requires it.

## Hosting

Accounts and synced progress are hosted by Supabase Inc. as our processor, in a data centre in [region to be confirmed, for example the EU (Frankfurt)]. Supabase stores this data on our behalf and may not use it for its own purposes. Like every server, it also processes technical data such as your IP address in order to handle requests.

## Calendar of the day

To show the saint, feast or readings of the day, the app requests a public calendar, depending on the church you chose: orthocal.info (Eastern Orthodox), api.coptic.io (Coptic Orthodox) or calapi.inadiutorium.cz (Catholic). The request contains only the date and, for the Catholic calendar, the language. No account information is sent. These services do see your IP address, as with every internet request. The result is kept on your device so the app also works offline.

## Reminders

Prayer-hour reminders and gentle reminders when you have not opened the app for a while are scheduled on your device itself. No server is involved. Android asks your permission for notifications, and you can turn every reminder off in Me → Settings.

## Links and sharing

When you share a verse, open a link (for example to Wikipedia), send us an e-mail or open the donation page, this happens through another app of your choice, such as your browser or e-mail app. That app's own privacy policy then applies.

## Why we use your data

We use your e-mail address and progress only to provide the app: to sign you in, keep your progress safe and make it available on your devices (performing our agreement with you). Notifications are only sent if you allow them (your consent). We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account and synced progress for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your account and all synced progress are deleted immediately from our database. Backups made by our hosting provider are overwritten within [period to be confirmed]. If you sign out, your progress is removed from that device but remains in your account.

## Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to receive a copy (data portability), to restrict or object to its use, and to withdraw consent. You can delete your account and all synced data yourself in Me → Settings → Delete account. For a copy of your data or any other request, write to the address above; we will answer within one month. You may also file a complaint with your data protection authority; in the Netherlands that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

## Children

AGAPE is meant for a general audience. If you are younger than 16, please ask a parent or guardian before you create an account [age limit to be confirmed by legal review].
